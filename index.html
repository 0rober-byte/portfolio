About me

I’m Robertino, a graphic designer with 15+ years of experience.

I help brands communicate, connect and grow through strategic and thoughtful visual design.

My name is Robertino Marrazzo.

I am a 39-year-old Argentine citizen and a naturalized Italian citizen currently residing in Italy for the past year and a half, and I hold a Bachelor’s Degree in Visual Communication Design.

I work as a freelance designer, enhancing brands and social media through strategic, well-crafted visual solutions.

I have an efficient, self-motivated, and collaborative profile. I value teamwork and the exchange of knowledge as key drivers of both creative excellence and personal growth.
